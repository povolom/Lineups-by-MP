A research desk for daily fantasy.

Bookmark players with their salaries, see who is worth their price tonight, build lineups under the cap and keep a ledger of results.

It never connects to Yahoo: salaries are typed or pasted by hand. Projections are estimates, not picks.

How I'm building it

  1. Watchlist and value board on sample data, with tests.
  2. Real stats from free sources, cached and rate-limited.
  3. Lineup builder, odds, live points and a ledger.
